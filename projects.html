Digital sovereignty

↗

A community-driven directory for digital sovereignty: find GDPR-compliant, European-owned alternatives to Big Tech. The project also includes a community hub, blog and contribution path.

Syntag

↗

A browser-based AI content disclosure tool. Users can add labels to images, video, audio, PDFs and ZIP files while the file stays local in the browser.

Machine learning · Cognitive science

Human Things

↗

Investigates whether human similarity judgments can improve machine-vision embeddings by fine-tuning ResNet-50 models on THINGS object images and human odd-one-out data.

↗

An end-to-end research pipeline for collecting and analysing publicly shared ChatGPT conversations from Reddit, including semantic, sentiment, linguistic and syntactic alignment.
