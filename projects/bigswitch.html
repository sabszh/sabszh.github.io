BigSwitch.eu

A community-driven directory for digital sovereignty: find GDPR-compliant, European-owned alternatives to Big Tech.

What it does

BigSwitch.eu helps people discover alternatives across analytics, cloud infrastructure, collaboration, communication, email, messaging and translation.

How it works

The directory combines transparent information with community participation, making it easier to compare and contribute to a more sovereign European technology landscape.

Why it matters

It turns an abstract question about digital sovereignty into a practical starting point: what could you use instead?
